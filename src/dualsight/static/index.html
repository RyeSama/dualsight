Under the hood

Two models. One clean contract.

The vision model is a sensor. The reasoning model is the analyst. DualSight is the wire between them — bounded, cached, and honest about uncertainty.

01

Perceive

A question-agnostic pass extracts OCR, objects, layout, colors, chart values, and explicit uncertainties. The result is cached by image hash.

profile=auto · detail=standard
02

Render evidence

Evidence becomes a fenced, nonce-tagged block with a clear “untrusted data” boundary. Images are stripped before the text model is called.

<<<VISUAL_EVIDENCE …>>>
03

Reason

Your existing text model answers from the evidence. Token usage, latency, cache hits, and degradation are returned in one metadata block.
